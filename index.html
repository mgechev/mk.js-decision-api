    Controls

    • Arrows: move / jump / squat · Shift: block
    • A: high punch · S: low punch · D: low kick · F: high kick
    • Down + A: uppercut · Back + F: spin kick · Jump + A/S/D/F: jump attack
    • Space: restart
    • Stand where you calibrated. If detection drifts, return to your neutral stance and press Recalibrate.
    • Step left / right of the dashed lines to walk. Jump to jump (jump while stepping to the side to jump forward/back).
    • Punch out to the side: fist at head height = high punch, at chest = low punch.
    • Fist straight up above your head = uppercut. Both fists in front of your face = block.
    • Raise a knee = low kick, raise a leg to hip height = high kick. Squat to squat; squat + punch = low punch.
    • The keyboard keeps working alongside the camera.
